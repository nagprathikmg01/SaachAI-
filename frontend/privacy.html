Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

At SachhAI, we prioritize the privacy and security of candidate evaluation and speech analysis data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and process data when utilizing the Interview Integrity Co-Pilot.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We process voice audio streams, closed caption text, and metadata during active interview sessions. This data is collected solely to detect linguistic style anomalies, plagiarism markers, and to calculate credibility assessment scores in real time.

2. Speech Analysis & AI Tools

Transcriptions and audio data are analyzed locally using heuristic metrics and machine learning models. External API calls (such as Deepgram for speech-to-text and OpenAI for technical checks) are performed securely. We do not use your voice data or transcripts to train public AI models.

3. Data Retention & Control

Interviews are stored securely in our database and can only be accessed by authorized HR recruiters or administrators of the conducting organization. Session records can be fully deleted at any time from the administration dashboard.
